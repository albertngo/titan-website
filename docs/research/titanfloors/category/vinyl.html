Vinyl Flooring Questions?

We've got the scoop—because your flooring dilemmas deserve a little vinyl twist!

What is vinyl flooring made of?
Vinyl flooring is a synthetic superstar, composed of multiple layers including a backing, a core layer, a printed design, and a clear protective top layer. It’s like the Swiss Army knife of flooring!
Is vinyl flooring durable?
You bet! Vinyl is tough enough to handle foot traffic, spills, and stains without breaking a sweat. It’s the floor that can handle your busy life with ease.
Can vinyl flooring be installed over existing floors?
Absolutely! Vinyl can often be laid over existing floors, as long as they’re clean, flat, and smooth. Just make sure your old floor isn’t throwing a party of its own!
How do I clean and maintain vinyl flooring?
Keep it simple—sweep or vacuum regularly, and mop with a gentle cleaner. Vinyl’s low-maintenance nature means you won’t need to call in a floor-cleaning squad.
Is vinyl flooring water-resistant?
You got it! Vinyl is great for moisture-prone areas like kitchens and bathrooms. Just don’t leave it floating in a puddle—remember, it’s water-resistant, not waterproof.
Can I install vinyl flooring myself?
Yes, if you’re up for a DIY adventure! Many vinyl options feature a click-lock installation, making it a breeze for even the most enthusiastic weekend warriors.
How long does vinyl flooring last?
With proper care, vinyl flooring can last 10-20 years. It’s the long-lasting friend who won’t let you down, and won’t ask for frequent updates or refinishing.
